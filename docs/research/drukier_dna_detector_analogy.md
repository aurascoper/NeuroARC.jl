## 1. Central Transferable Principle and Methodological Corrections

Drukier et al. (2015) propose recording nuclear-recoil trajectories via strand cleavage in ordered arrays of single-stranded nucleic acids (ssDNA or bacterial 23S-rRNA). In their baseline hanging-curtain architecture ([1206.6809v2.pdf](file:///Users/aurascoper/Downloads/1206.6809v2.pdf), Section III, p. 9), bundle barcodes localize tracks to $1\ \mu\text{m} \times 1\ \mu\text{m}$ in the $x$-$y$ plane while base sequencing provides $\sim 0.7\text{ nm}$ resolution along the $z$-axis. O'Hare et al. (2022) advance this concept with Monte Carlo simulations of particle track topologies and angular resolution while emphasizing that key physical and biochemical readout properties still require laboratory validation.

The transferable software-engineering principle is not that `NeuroARC.jl` and the nucleic-acid detector are mathematically equivalent. It is:

> Design the observation and verification harness so that a candidate signal must leave a discriminating, localized, and auditable trace, and define in advance what makes that trace unacceptable.

### Two Mandatory Methodological Corrections

1. **Directional Symmetry Breaking Is Not $D_8$-Equivariance**:
   In Drukier et al. (2015), directional sensitivity exploits an expected physical directional distribution (the Galactic WIMP wind toward Cygnus producing a $\sim 10:1$ backward-to-forward recoil asymmetry and diurnal modulation). In `NeuroARC.jl`, equivariance ([`evaluate_equivariance`](file:///Users/aurascoper/.gemini/antigravity/scratch/NeuroARC.jl/src/verifier/loo.jl#L156-L248)) is an algebraic property of how a computation transforms under a specified group action ($f(g \cdot X) = g \cdot f(X)$ for $g \in D_8$). A benchmark can test both invariance/equivariance under $D_8$ and directional sensitivity, but evidence for one is never evidence for the other.
2. **The "One-and-Only-One" Gate Is a Design Analogy, Not a Demonstrated Isomorphism**:
   In the detector, the "one and only one" gold film criterion ([1206.6809v2.pdf](file:///Users/aurascoper/Downloads/1206.6809v2.pdf), p. 12) is motivated by the physical stopping ranges of $10\text{ keV}$ Au recoil nuclei ($\sim 20\text{ nm}$ in Mylar) versus $5\text{ MeV}$ background $\alpha$-particles ($\sim 30\ \mu\text{m}$ in Mylar). In `NeuroARC.jl`, a quarantine gate must be justified solely by its actual software data boundaries, information flow, and acceptance logic.

## 3. Three Actionable Software Experiments for `NeuroARC.jl`

### Experiment 1: Directional Discrimination vs. Group Equivariance Benchmark
- **Approved `EquivarianceEvidence` Redesign**:
  Extend [`EquivarianceEvidence`](file:///Users/aurascoper/.gemini/antigravity/scratch/NeuroARC.jl/src/types.jl#L129-L133) with explicit transformation-set metadata rather than a fixed 4-symbol subgroup label:
  - `requested_group::Symbol`: The symmetry claim under test (e.g., `:D8`, `:C4`, `:D1_refl_v`, `:D1_refl_h`, `:C1`).
  - `tested_transforms::Vector{Symbol}`: Exact transformations evaluated (`:id`, `:rot90`, `:rot180`, `:rot270`, `:refl_h`, `:refl_v`, `:refl_main_diag`, `:refl_anti_diag`).
  - `passed_transforms::Vector{Symbol}`: Transformations for which the commutation test passed.
  - `failed_transforms::Vector{Symbol}`: Transformations that produced a testable, genuine mismatch.
  - `untestable_transforms::Vector{Symbol}`: Transformations that could not be meaningfully evaluated.
  - `status::Symbol`: Retain `:passed`, `:failed`, or `:untestable`, scoped to `requested_group`.
- **Protocol**: Fix a synthetic benchmark of 40 tasks across 4 known ground-truth classes (10 tasks each, $N = 3$ training pairs per task):
  1. **Class A (Full $D_8$ + Color Equivariant)**: `MarkerLineReflectionRule(c; axis_candidate=:point)`, `ArcGeyserRule(c; directions=:cardinal)`, `ArcGeyserRule(c; directions=:all8)`.
  2. **Class B (Chiral $C_4$ Rotational Equivariant, Reflection-Antisymmetric)**: `ArcGyrismRule(c; angle_degrees=90, clockwise=true)` and `angle_degrees=270` on multi-pixel L/T-polyominoes (testing the fix to [`circulate_gyre`](file:///Users/aurascoper/.gemini/antigravity/scratch/NeuroARC.jl/src/rules/gyrism.jl#L69-L98)).
  3. **Class C (Directional / Partial-Reflection Rules)**: `ArcGeyserRule(c; directions=:down)` (which preserves left-right reflection `:refl_v` while failing rotations and vertical flip), `:up`, `:left`, `:right`, and `MarkerLineReflectionRule(c; axis_candidate=:vertical)`.
  4. **Class D (Isotropic Noise / Overfit Nulls)**: Random cell-lookup tables and corrupted outputs matching $2/3$ training pairs.
- **Acceptance Threshold**:
  - Class A: `0.0` false-rejection rate (`:passed` on `requested_group = :D8` with all 8 transforms in `passed_transforms`, plus non-landmark foreground color permutations).
  - Class B: `0.0` pixel loss under `circulate_gyre`; under `requested_group = :C4`, passes all 4 rotations (`:id`, `:rot90`, `:rot180`, `:rot270`) with `status == :passed`, while under `requested_group = :D8`, the 4 reflections are recorded in `failed_transforms` (never hidden as `:untestable`).
  - Class C: Exact transform sets recorded in `passed_transforms` / `failed_transforms` / `untestable_transforms`, while `execute_d8_fold_consensus_weave` recovers the exact conjugate orientation on held-out folds.
  - Class D: `0.0` false-positive acceptance rate (`100%` `:failed`, never labeled `:untestable`).
- **Failure Receipt**: Emit structured `EquivarianceBenchmarkReceipt` recording per-class FPR, FNR, and transform-level results.

### Experiment 2: Quarantine Integrity & Fallback-Isolation Suite
- **Protocol**: Construct 6 adversarial candidate families attempting to cross compartment or data boundaries:
  1. **Overfitted Parametric Geyser/Gyre on $N-1$ LOO**: A rule whose parameter fits fold $N$ only if not refit on folds $1 \dots N-1$, testing concrete `refit_rule_parameters(::ArcGeyserRule, ...)` and `refit_rule_parameters(::ArcGyrismRule, ...)` as well as refitting inside `execute_d8_fold_consensus_weave`.
  2. **Unsealed Quarantine Row Injection**: Passing `test_quarantine_sealed = false` into `append_sealed_row!` and `verify_ledger_cryptographic_seal`, verifying immediate `:quarantine_breach` rejection (with the hardcoded `true` removed).
  3. **Mid-Borrow C-ABI TOCTOU Mutation**: Mutating `buf[1, 1]` inside a malicious `GraftNode` during `execute_c_abi_zpd_graft!`, verifying `:fnv1a_toctou_post_mismatch` rejection and `lock.state[] == UInt8(PYTHON_OWNED)` recovery.
  4. **Non-Contiguous C-ABI Stride & Out-of-Range DSL ID**: Passing `stride_row = 2` or `dsl_operator_id = UInt16(999) > VERIFIED_DSL_MAX_ID`, verifying preflight rejection before `unsafe_wrap`.
  5. **High-Partial-Score Fallback Bleed**: Feeding a `Rejected` candidate with `partial_score = 0.99` into the submission router and `ComputeBudgetScheduler`, verifying zero lineage empowerment (`weights` unchanged) and zero leakage into `Accepted` submission slots.
  6. **Unified Verifier Entrypoint Check**: Verifying that `depolarize_membrane` enforces `evaluate_loo` and returns a typed `AbstractVerdict` (no `Inf` sentinels).
- **Acceptance Threshold**: `100%` (`6/6`) adversarial families rejected with exact expected `verdict.reason` and `failed_gates`, and `0` fallback acceptances.

### Experiment 3: Deterministic Forensic Replay from Audit Snapshots
- **Protocol**:
  1. Implement a versioned, canonical serialization (`seal_schema_version = "rev3.v2"`) of every field in `TaskAuditRow` (`task_id`, `git_commit_hash`, `dsl_operator_ids`, `candidate_ast_sha256`, `ast_expr`, `preflight_subtype_ok`, `runtime_typeassert_ok`, `train_pairs_count`, `train_exact_matches`, `train_max_residual`, `loo_folds_total`, `loo_folds_passed`, `equivariance_status`, `test_quarantine_sealed`, `verdict_status`, `verdict_reason`, `failed_gates`, `partial_score`, `wall_time_ns`, `memory_bytes`, `prev_row_seal_sha256`) in `append_sealed_row!`, `verify_ledger_cryptographic_seal`, and `verify_audit_seal.jl`.
  2. Implement a safe, allowlisted DSL parser (`parse_allowlisted_candidate_ast`) for replay that reconstructs only allowlisted rule and graft constructors from `ast_expr` without ever evaluating arbitrary Julia code (`eval` forbidden).
  3. Record 20 candidate evaluations (both `Accepted` and `Rejected` across `MarkerLineReflectionRule`, `ArcGeyserRule`, `ArcGyrismRule`, and 3-stage `GraftNode` chains) to a temporary `task_audit_table.parquet.jls`, reload in a clean state, and deterministically replay each candidate.
  4. Mutate every single material field one at a time in negative tampering tests, plus verify that non-allowlisted `ast_expr` strings are rejected by the replay parser.
- **Acceptance Threshold**: `100%` (`20/20`) replayed candidates reproduce the exact recorded verification outputs and `row_seal_sha256`; `100%` of single-field tamper mutations and disallowed AST expressions are rejected.
