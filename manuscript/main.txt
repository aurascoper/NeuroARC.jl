                                                 Abstract
         Search-based solvers for the Abstraction and Reasoning Corpus (ARC) fail in three recurrent
     ways: multi-step compositions crash at runtime when intermediate grid types disagree; candidate
     verifiers return numeric sentinels (Inf, -1.0) that leak into beam scores; and untestable invariants
     are silently treated as passed or failed. We present NeuroARC.jl, a zero-dependency Julia package
     that addresses these failure modes through three typed layers: (1) a constructive evidence calculus
     (TransjectionCalculus) that separates operational three-way verdicts (Accepted, Rejected,
     Incomplete) from sealed proof and refutation certificates (Witness, Refutation, Unresolved)
     verified at construction time; (2) a preflight type-checking contract (HyphalGraftingContract)
     that rejects incompatible operator chains via Out(A) <: In(B) before executing any grid
     transformation; and (3) a deterministic candidate verifier combining exact training replay,
     informative leave-one-out parameter refitting, and a three-valued equivariance test under the
     eight symmetries of the square (D4 , order 8) and background-preserving color permutations. The
     package is verified across 652 unit-test assertions and enforces a four-level scientific achievement
     taxonomy separating implemented interfaces from experimentally supported claims.

Keywords: Abstraction and Reasoning Corpus; program synthesis; test-time training; criterial
causation; constructive verification; typed composition; Julia.


1    Introduction and Epistemic Architecture Boundaries
The Abstraction and Reasoning Corpus (ARC) challenges current artificial intelligence architectures
by requiring broad general intelligence across discrete grid environments [2]. Typical approaches rely
on large language model code generation, brute-force domain specific language (DSL) enumeration,
or end-to-end neural policy search. However, existing implementations often suffer from three
systemic defects: (1) lack of type safety during dynamic program composition, leading to silent
runtime type coercions or uninformative exceptions; (2) use of numerical sentinel values like Inf
or -1.0 to signal candidate failure, which frequently leak into numerical optimizers and distort
beam search scoring; and (3) conflation of distinct epistemic categories, wherein speculative search
heuristics are mislabeled as verified mathematical theorems.
    To resolve these defects, we present NeuroARC.jl, a zero-dependency Julia package that formal-
izes the verification and typed-composition contract motivated by our ARC Prize 2026 competition
runs. A central design principle of our research program is strict epistemological stratification across
three distinct research tracks:

      typed composition (HyphalGraftingContract), leave-one-out verification, and pedagogical
      scaffolding.
    By maintaining this separation, NeuroARC.jl formalizes candidate verification and operator
composition as machine-checked computational contracts without conflating the standalone Ju-
lia formalization with the Python leaderboard submission notebooks or foundational perceptual
theorems.

    1. Stage t1 (Setting of Informational Criteria): The controller configures acceptance

2.1   Operational Three-Way Policy versus Formal Constructive Proof Calculus
Because intuitionistic logic admits no finite-valued truth-table semantics [5], we explicitly distinguish
an operational three-way verification policy (Accepted, Rejected, and Incomplete status
enumeration at the CriterialVerdict layer) from a formally constructive proof calculus
implemented as EvidenceStatus with Witness{T, C} (C <: AbstractProofTerm), Refutation{T,
C} (C <: AbstractRefutationTerm), and Unresolved{T}. An unguarded certificate::Any
field would allow Witness(prop, nothing) or Witness(prop, true) to masquerade as a proof
term. Instead, Witness{T, C} and Refutation{T, C} constrain C <: AbstractCertificate
and invoke check_proof_term and check_refutation_term inside their inner constructors at
instantiation time. Generic fallback methods check_proof_term(::Any, ::Any) = false and
check_refutation_term(::Any, ::Any) = false fail closed on any unverified certificate subtype.
For StructuredProofTerm and StructuredRefutationTerm, the constructor check verifies the
structural hash seal, proposition alignment, and internal consistency of the recorded fields (such as
measured value versus threshold and margin arithmetic for threshold and bandpass gates, subtype
relation out_type <: in_type for subtype gates, recursive child-certificate validity for composite
gates, and an optional recheck closure when supplied by evaluate_gate_constructive), while
Unresolved{T} requires non-empty missing_prerequisites symbols when neither certificate is
available. Constructive gate evaluation (evaluate_gate_constructive) and Brouwer-Heyting-
Kolmogorov (BHK) combinators (conjunction_evidence and disjunction_evidence) enforce
the strict epistemic invariant that an unavailable prerequisite yields Unresolved{T} and is never
coerced into a Refutation{T} counterexample.
    To ground continuous-to-discrete sensitivity and constructive evidence semantics without ambi-
guity, we contrast three distinct mathematical universes for smooth or nilpotent infinitesimals and
constructive logic:

  1. Synthetic Differential Geometry (SDG) in Smooth Toposes: Moerdijk and Reyes [9]
     construct intuitionistic topos models governed by the Kock-Lawvere microaffineness axiom [1,9],
     where every function on the nilsquare infinitesimal object D = {d ∈ R : d2 = 0} is uniquely
     affine.

  2. Classical-Modal Interpretation of Smooth Infinitesimal Analysis: Hellman and
     Shapiro [6] formulate a classical-modal reinterpretation of nilsquare identity, demonstrating
     how validity depends critically on internal versus external quantifier scope.

  3. Classical-Logic Fermat Reals: Giordano [4] develops the ring of Fermat reals within strictly
     classical logic, proving that nilpotent infinitesimals do not automatically force intuitionistic
     logic and making the choice of mathematical universe mathematically consequential.

Maintenance, Marking Critical Features, Frustration Control, and Demonstration), managing state
transitions across Focus, Frustration, and Panic modes.


3     Hyphal Grafting and Deep Composition Contracts
Complex ARC reasoning rules rarely arise as atomic primitives; they are formed by composing
modular operators such as connected component extractors, spatial transformers, and stencil
stampers. We formalize this composition through the biological metaphor of hyphal grafting.

3.1    Static Preflight Subtype Checking
Let A and B denote two transformation nodes encapsulated within parametric types:
                      A :: GraftNode{InA , OutA },       B :: GraftNode{InB , OutB }                     (1)
Before connecting A and B, the system evaluates Julia’s subtype lattice relation:
                                               OutA <: InB                                               (2)
If this condition is violated, the edge is rejected during preflight validation with zero executions of
the underlying functions A.f or B.f . This resolves a significant source of latency in large search
graphs by pruning invalid compositional branches before dispatch.

3.2    Runtime Intermediate Boundary Enforcement
Static subtyping guarantees that declared signatures are compatible; however, it does not guarantee
that an arbitrary implementation A.f strictly respects its declared return type for all edge cases. To
prevent unverified types from propagating across intermediate pipeline boundaries, the composed
execution wrapper is defined as:
                                      (B ◦ A)(x) = B(A(x))                                          (3)
where each node call wrapper (N )(x) enforces:
                                   Core.typeassert(N.f (x), OutN )                                       (4)
This ensures that any intermediate contract violation immediately triggers an auditable TypeError
at the exact boundary of failure, rather than corrupting subsequent pipeline stages.

4.1        Verification Invariants
Before any candidate is admitted into the verified execution pool, it must satisfy three deterministic
gates:
  1. Train Exact Reconstruction: The candidate transformation must reconstruct 100% of
     cells across all training demonstration output grids (Yi = f (Xi )) with maximum cell residual
     equal to 0.0.

  2. Informative Leave-One-Out (LOO) Evaluation: Cross-validation across demonstration
     pairs is evaluated to detect parameter overfitting. When a rule contains free parameters, LOO
     folds are marked informative; parameter-free macros require verified cross-task firing to avoid
     single-task memorization.

  3. Equivariance Gating: Where task semantics exhibit geometric invariance, the candidate
     must commute with the eight symmetries of the square (D4 , order 8) and background-preserving
     color permutations. If a candidate depends on directional or absolute spatial coordinates,
     equivariance is flagged as untestable, leading to an explicit Incomplete evaluation rather
     than a false positive certification.
The package exports seven AbstractGridRule subtypes: three rules with complete execution meth-
ods (MarkerLineReflectionRule, ArcGeyserRule, and ArcGyrismRule) and four typed extension
stubs (GeodesicBarrierFloodRule, CorridorWireTraceRule, PeriodicStampWithPriorityRule,
and ExactCoverPolyominoRule) that throw RuleNotImplementedError and evaluate to :incomplete
under verify_candidate rather than being silently treated as training mismatches.

4.2        Epistemic Limits of Verification and Scientific Achievement Taxonomy
The verifier certifies compliance with observed demonstration contracts and structural invariants; it
does not and cannot certify ground-truth accuracy on held-out test grids. Post-run test evaluation
is conducted strictly through isolated scoring harnesses against quarantined held-out labels after
solver processes terminate.
    To enforce strict alignment between constructive proof terms and scientific claims, the verifier
separates the operational status policy (Accepted, Rejected, Incomplete) from the formal con-
structive evidence hierarchy (EvidenceStatus: Witness{T}, Refutation{T}, and Unresolved{T}).
Furthermore, ScientificClaimAudit{T} and audit_scientific_claim_boundary audit every
scientific proposition against four ordered achievement levels:
  1. Implemented Interface (IMPLEMENTED_INTERFACE): Typed structural signatures and com-
     positional contracts are defined and type-checked.

                                                                                                                                          5
    2. Verified Numerical Component (VERIFIED_NUMERICAL_COMPONENT): Algorithmic and
       numerical invariants pass deterministic unit and leave-one-out verification suites with re-
       checkable witness certificates.

    3. Validated Physical Model (VALIDATED_PHYSICAL_MODEL): Physical or dynamical predic-
       tions are validated against independent reference benchmarks.

    4. Experimentally Supported Empirical Claim (EXPERIMENTALLY_SUPPORTED_CLAIM): Em-
       pirical claims are supported by direct experimental replication and held-out benchmark
       evaluation.

If a proposition’s claimed_level exceeds its achieved_level or its underlying evidence is a
Refutation{T}, audit_scientific_claim_boundary immediately emits a Refutation{T} coun-
tercertificate. Conversely, if experimental prerequisites or independent verification records are not
yet available, it emits Unresolved{T} recording the missing prerequisite symbols without coercing
absent prerequisites into a refutation.

4.3    Structural Analogy: Symmetry Breaking and Single-Compartment Quaran-
       tine
The verifier’s gate ordering is structurally analogous to directional recoil detectors in particle
astrophysics [3], where enforcing geometric symmetry against a reference axis reduces the sample
size required for background rejection across small event counts. Analogously, our leave-one-out
and equivariance gates isolate each candidate evaluation inside a single compartment and record
deterministic hash receipts so rejected or incomplete candidates never leak into downstream search.


5     Package Architecture and Zero-Dependency Core Isolation
To maintain high execution velocity and reproducibility on minimal environments, NeuroARC.jl
enforces zero-dependency core isolation. The core library relies exclusively on the Julia standard
library, implementing lightweight internal parsers and memory buffers without external binary
dependencies.

5.1    Standard Julia Package Skeleton
The repository adheres to the standard Julia package layout:

    • Project.toml: Declares package UUID
      (a3f5a15a-93e1-4bd8-99e6-0563b78be05c), version bounds on Julia (1.10 to 1.13), and
      conditional extension dependencies.

    • src/NeuroARC.jl: Exposes public APIs for grid representations, component analysis, criterial
      calculus, and hyphal grafting contracts.

    • ext/: Houses conditional package extensions that load only when external packages are present
      in the environment:

         • NeuroARCMetalExt.jl: GPU acceleration for Apple Silicon via Metal.jl.
         • NeuroARCJACCExt.jl: Cross-hardware portability via JACC.jl.
         • NeuroARCTuringExt.jl: Bayesian MCMC calibration via Turing.jl.

6     Discussion, Competition Governance, and Repository Lifecycle
A central operational question addressed in this milestone is the relationship between our Python
competition submission notebooks (Track 1) and the standalone NeuroARC.jl reference package
(Track 3).

6.1    Empirical Context and Decontamination Governance
Three engineering constraints shaped the lifecycle from internal development to open-source release:

    1. Audit and Specification Invariants: The anti-solipsism framework, the deterministic
       verifier threat model, and the constructive certificate calculus separate verified numerical
       components (VERIFIED_NUMERICAL_COMPONENT) from unverified empirical hypotheses.

    2. Kaggle ARC-AGI-2 Decontamination Governance: In our audit of the upstream Python
       dsl.py module (164 top-level FunctionDef nodes), 73 definitions (72 unique runtime keys
       due to a duplicate solve_b9e38dc0 definition) were task-keyed solve_<8hex> lookup tables
       rather than generalizable operators, leaving 91 reusable grid helpers. Exact-match regex
       stripping in the submitted clean run removed 71 runtime keys and leaked one renamed alias
       (solve_9385bd28_generalized) through a default-argument binding, collapsing accuracy
       from 39.17 on the public evaluation set (scored locally) to 0.00 on the hidden leaderboard.
       That failure motivated the constructive verification gates formalized here.

    3. Cross-Language Boundary between Python and Julia: The Kaggle ARC-AGI-2
       notebooks run in Python on Kaggle’s offline four-L4 GPU environment within the twelve-hour
       wall-clock limit (reaching 31.94 and 29.72 under our triple-pass NVARC test-time training
       schedule, and 30.28 on S1, 28.47 on the triangulated replicate, and also 28.47 on S5 with the
       Python sidecar). NeuroARC.jl is the standalone Julia formalization of the sidecar’s verification
       and typed-composition contract rather than the runtime engine executed inside the Kaggle
       submission kernels.

6.2    Open-Source Verification and Reproducibility
For the ARC Prize 2026 Paper Track release, the repository is packaged as a clean, zero-dependency
Julia codebase verified by:

    1. Deterministic execution of all 652 unit-test assertions via Pkg.test() across core types,
       constructive proof certificates, preflight grafting contracts, and text-cleanliness invariants.

    2. Automated Graphviz and Tectonic compilation of the manuscript and architectural diagrams
       (Figure B1 and Figure B2).
